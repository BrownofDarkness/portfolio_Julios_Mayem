# Modernize a school's online presence.

The school had an old site indexed on Google but out of date: no
photos, no working contact form, no way to pre-register. Most
enquiries still came in by phone.

I took the whole project on: **wireframes, visual identity,
responsive front in HTML/CSS/JavaScript**, then PHP/MySQL for the
forms and processing of pre-registrations. The server side is kept
deliberately simple — it's what the school could actually maintain.

On the retained test conditions, the site scores **Lighthouse above
90** on performance and accessibility, with a load time under
**1.5 seconds**.
