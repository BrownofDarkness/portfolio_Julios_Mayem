# Inspiring African youth towards academic and professional excellence.

<Figure
  src="/projects/afrishape-hero.png"

AfriShape is a web platform designed to inspire, guide and inform
young people towards academic and professional excellence. The goal:
to centralise resources, opportunities and reference career paths to
help them build their future with method and ambition.

I handled the design and front-end development: **information
architecture, visual identity, responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript**,
ensuring the platform stays fast and accessible even on limited
connections. The homepage presents the community's key figures and
immediately directs visitors to the most relevant resources.

<Callout kind="insight" label="What I take from it">
Building for a young African audience demands concrete choices: performance on degraded networks, message clarity, real accessibility — not checkbox items, but constraints that shape every design decision.
</Callout>
