# Inspiring African youth towards academic and professional excellence.

<Figure
  src="/projects/afrishape-hero.png"

AfriShape is a web platform designed to inspire, guide and inform
young people towards academic and professional excellence. It
centralises resources, opportunities and reference career paths to
help them build their future.

The platform was built by a team of developers. I contributed to the
**design** and to the **back-end development** with the **Django**
framework: data organisation, server-side logic and serving content
to the interface. The platform is live at
[afrishape.org](https://www.afrishape.org/).

<Callout kind="insight" label="What I take from it">
Working with others on a shared codebase demands rigour: shared conventions, a clear split of responsibilities, and a back end that stays readable for the whole team.
</Callout>
