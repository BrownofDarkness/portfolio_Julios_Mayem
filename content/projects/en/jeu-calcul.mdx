# Make mental-math practice engaging, without hiding its demand.

<Figure
  ratio="hero"
  num="01"
  caption="Gameplay screen — exercise, live score, time constraint (Android, 2022).">
  Three stacked screenshots: home, exercise in progress, records table.
</Figure>

I used to tutor 9th-grade students in mathematics. The ones who
stumbled on mental arithmetic weren't short on intelligence — they
were short on deliberate practice. A well-designed exercise, short
and scored, can change the relationship to the subject.

I designed and coded the Android app: exercise-generation engine
producing varied exercises, scoring logic, adaptive
difficulty progression, tactile feedback animations, local SQLite
storage of records. The app stays deliberately lightweight —
students' phones have little free space to spare on a game.

This project doesn't claim to replace a teacher. It starts from a
simple idea: short, regular practice beats a long session now and
then.
