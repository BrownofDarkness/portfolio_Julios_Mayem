# Centralise sales, stock and margins in a single tool.

GESCOM is a sales management system. It brings the till, invoicing,
margin tracking and stock management together in one tool, with
alerts when an item is about to run out.

I handled the design, modelling and development. The information
system was modelled with **MERISE**, from the conceptual model to
the logical model above, then implemented on a **MySQL** relational
database. I then developed the till and invoicing modules, the
margin calculations and the out-of-stock alert mechanism.

MERISE suited the problem: GESCOM is first and foremost about data —
who sells what, at what price, with how much stock left. A clean data
model then keeps processing simple: margins follow from the recorded
prices, and the out-of-stock alert comes down to comparing current
stock with a threshold.
