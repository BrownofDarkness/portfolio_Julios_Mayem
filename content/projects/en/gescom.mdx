# Centralize sales, stock, and margins for a small business.

The shopkeeper was running the till by hand: notebooks,
gut-feeling margin calculations, inventory gaps we could no longer
explain. GESCOM had to absorb the daily register, invoicing, and
stock tracking — without forcing a rewrite of his habits.

I modelled the information system with **MERISE**, designed the
MySQL relational database, developed the point-of-sale and
invoicing modules, and the margin calculations and stock-shortage
alerts. The catalogue scaled to **1,200+ SKUs** with no visible
degradation.

After go-live, **undocumented inventory gaps vanished within the
first month** — which, in a small business, translates directly
into cash flow.
