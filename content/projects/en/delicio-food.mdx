# Find a nearby restaurant, filtered by what you actually want.

Delicio Food started from a plain observation: search apps didn't
help you decide between ten equivalent suggestions. What was missing
was a fast filter and a **map that refreshes the instant** the user
changes a criterion.

I built the app with **Flutter and Dart**, integrated Google Maps
and GPS geolocation, and organised SQLite persistence for favourites
and history — so the app remains usable on a degraded network.
Distance calculation and filtering run under **300 ms** on tested
devices.

The experience is tuned to stay fluid (up to **60 frames per second**
on tested Android devices), which matters for street use, one hand
on the phone.
