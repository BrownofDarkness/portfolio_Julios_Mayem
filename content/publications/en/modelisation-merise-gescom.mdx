## Why MERISE

GESCOM had to centralise sales, invoicing, margin tracking and stock
management. That is first and foremost a data problem: who sells
what, at what price, with how much stock left. MERISE, which clearly
separates data from processing, fits it well.

## From conceptual to physical

The approach has three levels:

1. **Conceptual data model** — business entities (item, customer,
   sale, sale line, supplier, stock movement) and their associations,
   independent of any technology.
2. **Logical data model** — the conceptual model translated into
   relational tables, primary keys and foreign keys.
3. **Physical model** — the MySQL database itself: column types,
   indexes and integrity constraints.

## What the model made possible

A clean model makes processing simple to write: margins follow
directly from the recorded purchase and sale prices, and the
out-of-stock alert comes down to comparing current stock with a
per-item threshold.

## MERISE or 2TUP?

KKBSECU, by contrast, was modelled with 2TUP and UML: a system where
behaviour (capture, recognition, alerts) matters more than data
structure. The method follows the nature of the problem.
