## Context

During my internship at Sygalin SAS (2023), every new version of the
Flutter mobile app was built, signed and distributed by hand. Every
manual step is one more source of error and slows down each release.

## The pipeline

The Codemagic pipeline automatically chains:

1. **Trigger** on every change pushed to the repository.
2. **Build** of the Flutter app for Android and iOS.
3. **Automatic signing**, with certificates stored in Codemagic
   rather than on a developer's machine.
4. **Internal distribution** through TestFlight and the Play Store
   internal testing track.

## What I take away

A delivery pipeline is not a luxury reserved for large teams. On a
small team, automating signing and distribution frees up time for
the code and makes every release reproducible.
